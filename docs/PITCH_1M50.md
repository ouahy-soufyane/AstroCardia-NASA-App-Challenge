# Astrocardia — 1 minute 50 second pitch

Imagine an astronaut feeling unwell while their crew is far from a hospital. The medical team on Earth needs clear information, and the astronaut needs a simple way to share it.

That is why we created Astrocardia. It is a health-monitoring web app with two views: one for astronauts and one for doctors.

Astronauts can see their latest health readings, record their weight, height, and daily urine output, report physical or emotional concerns, and message their medical team. Doctors can view their assigned crew, search astronaut records, and check each person’s latest information.

The dashboard also includes a short animated demonstration. It shows health readings changing from green, to orange, to red, so a jury can quickly understand how an alert might appear. AstroCare AI has a narrow job: it summarizes the demo readings as a status and directs the astronaut to the medical officer. It does not diagnose or recommend medicine.

Our sample workbook contains one hundred fictional astronaut records. We also train a small JavaScript model to sort generated examples into three demonstration status groups. This is a learning prototype, not a medical tool, and its generated data does not prove real-world accuracy.

Astrocardia’s goal is simple: make it easier for astronauts to share health information and for medical teams to spot when they should follow up. With real medical review, secure data handling, and testing, the prototype could be developed further. Thank you.
